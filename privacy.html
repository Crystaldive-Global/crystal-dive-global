Privacy Policy - Crystal Dive Global

Last updated: 16 November 2026

1. Who We Are

Crystal Dive Global provides educational gem screening tools. Our website is https://crystaldive-global.github.io/crystal-dive-global/

2. What Data We Collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data inside the app. The app does not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, photos, or contacts. The SG calculators run only on your device. Your weights and results are not sent to us and are not stored on any server.

3. Third-Party Services

We do not use advertising trackers, analytics that identify you, or data-selling services inside the app. When you tap a link to our website or to WhatsApp, you will leave the app and those services will apply their own privacy policies.

4. Payments

Payments are not taken inside the app. All payments and purchases are handled on our official website. Please read the payment provider's policy on our website before paying.

5. Children

Our app is an educational tool for general audiences. It is not designed to collect data from children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and update the link in the app.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us via our website: https://crystaldive-global.github.io/crystal-dive-global/ or email: support@crystaldiveglobal.com
