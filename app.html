10. 📖 How To Do SG Test Guide

1: Calibrate 0.01g scale.
2: Weigh dry stone.
3: Fill cup distilled water, tare.
4: Hang stone with thread, fully in water, do NOT touch cup.
5: SG = Dry ÷ (Dry - Water).
6: Repeat twice.

18. ❓ FAQ + ⚖️ How To Weigh Correctly

Why SG off? Scale not 0.01g, stone touching cup, bubbles, metal setting.

How to weigh in water? Thin thread, fully submerged, not touching cup, no bubbles.

Jewelry without removing stone? Use Jewelry Test - enter metal type + metal weight.

Is SG enough? No. Strong guide, but use checklist + lab for final proof.
