📖 How To Do SG Test Guide - Standard Method

Zero scale with calibration weight • Use 0.01g scale • Use distilled water • Measure water temperature • Use thin thread, weigh thread in water separately • Stone fully submerged, not touching sides or bottom • Remove all bubbles with soft brush • Weigh air, then water • Repeat second time • If two SG results differ by more than 0.05, re-test • SG = Air / (Air - Water corrected for thread and water density)

❓ FAQ + ⚖️ How To Weigh Correctly

Why is SG different each time? Bubbles, temperature, thread weight, touching container.
Can SG prove real diamond? No alone. SG + hardness + thermal test + lab certificate.
Do I need lab? For high value stones, yes - GIA / IGI lab.
